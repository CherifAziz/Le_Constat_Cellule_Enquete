How to cite the database?

You are free to use the data for non-commercial use. We only ask you to cite the associated article: Bergeaud, A., Cette, G. and Lecat, R. (2016): "Productivity Trends in Advanced Countries between 1890 and 2012," Review of Income and Wealth, vol. 62(3), pages 420–444.

The database is regularly updated. Every update will be reported in this page. Do not hesitate to visit to check if new data are available.
